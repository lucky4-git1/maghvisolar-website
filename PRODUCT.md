# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Residential homeowners, apartment society management committee members (RWAs), factory owners, commercial enterprise directors, and agricultural landowners in Hyderabad, Telangana, and across South India looking to transition to solar energy, slash power bills, claim PM Surya Ghar subsidies, or install commercial solar micro-grids.

## Product Purpose

Meghvi Solar delivers turnkey solar engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) solutions. The website guides prospective clients through discovering the exact engineering system required, calculating instant savings and subsidies with live estimators, understanding DISCOM net-metering compliance, and scheduling free technical rooftop site surveys.

## Positioning

"Energy, engineered beautifully." Meghvi Solar stands apart from generic local solar installers by offering aerospace-grade structural integrity (150 km/h wind-rated hot-dip GI mounting), certified Tier-1 TOPCon cells, 5-year comprehensive free O&M, and direct liaisoning with TSSPDCL/TSNPDCL for net-metering and PM Surya Ghar government subsidy transfers.

## Operating Context

Headquartered in Chandanagar, Hyderabad, Meghvi Solar serves Hyderabad, Rangareddy, Sangareddy, Medchal, and neighboring southern states. The digital platform provides instant proposal calculations, loan vs. standard finance comparators, statutory subsidy tables, and frictionless WhatsApp and phone consultation pathways.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Turnkey EPC installation from 1 kW residential rooftop plants to 1 MW+ commercial solar micro-grids.
- Full DISCOM net-metering liaisoning and CEIG electrical inspections.
- Direct PM Surya Ghar and PM-KUSUM subsidy facilitation.
- Strict anti-fabrication standards: genuine equipment partnerships (Tata Power Solar, Adani, Waaree, Schneider, Havells, Sungrow) and genuine verified clients.

## Brand Commitments

Architectural, authoritative, transparent. The aesthetic combines clean mineral ivory backgrounds, deep graphite typography, restrained solar-amber accents, and tactile physical UI controls without generic AI-slop gradients or fake claims.

Three-word personality: **architectural, precise, reliable**.
